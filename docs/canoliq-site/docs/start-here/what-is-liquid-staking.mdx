# What Is Liquid Staking?

:::tip In one sentence
Staking normally locks your tokens; liquid staking gives you a tradable receipt token
(cCNPY) that keeps earning rewards while you stay free to move, sell, or use it elsewhere.
:::

## The problem: staking locks your capital

To help secure a proof-of-stake chain like Canopy, you **stake** tokens — you commit them
so validators can put value at risk against misbehavior, and in return you earn a share of
network rewards. The catch: staked tokens are **locked**. You cannot spend or trade them,
and unstaking takes an unbonding waiting period. Your capital is working, but it is frozen.

## The solution: a liquid receipt

**Liquid staking** solves this. When you deposit CNPY into canoLiq, the protocol stakes it
on your behalf and hands you a **receipt token, cCNPY**, that stands for your share of the
pooled, staked CNPY. Now:

- The underlying CNPY keeps **earning staking rewards**.
- Your **cCNPY is liquid** — you hold, transfer, or use it while the stake stays productive.
- When you want out, you **redeem** cCNPY for CNPY (after the unstaking window).

## Why cCNPY grows in value instead of paying you tokens

canoLiq does not drip reward tokens into your wallet. Instead, rewards flow back into the
shared pool, so each cCNPY comes to represent **more** CNPY over time. This is the
**exchange rate**:

```
exchange rate = total pooled CNPY ÷ total cCNPY supply   (rises as rewards arrive)
```

The first deposit mints cCNPY 1:1; after that you receive cCNPY at the current rate. Redeem
later and the rate has risen — that difference is your yield. See
[How It Works](../concepts/how-it-works.mdx) for the full walkthrough.

## The second token: CPLQ

cCNPY is about **yield**. canoLiq's second token, **CPLQ**, is about **governance** — a
fixed supply of 100M tokens whose holders vote on protocol parameters and can lock their
stake for extra voting weight. The two tokens do different jobs; see
[The Two Tokens](../concepts/two-tokens.mdx).

## Why canoLiq specifically

- **No lock-up drag** — your staked value stays usable as cCNPY.
- **Shared security** — it runs as a Canopy nested chain, borrowing Canopy's validators.
- **Community-owned** — CPLQ governance tunes the economics, funds a treasury, and runs a
  buyback; the protocol even tracks its own "[autonomy graduation](../advanced/autonomy-graduation.mdx)"
  metrics.
