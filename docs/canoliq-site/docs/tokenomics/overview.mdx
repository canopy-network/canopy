# Tokenomics Overview

**In one sentence:** canoLiq runs on a yield-bearing receipt token and a fixed-supply governance
token, and this page is the reference for both.

canoLiq manages two tokens alongside CNPY — a yield-bearing receipt and a fixed-supply
governance token. If you want the friendly version first, read
[The Two Tokens](../concepts/two-tokens.mdx); this page is the reference.

## cCNPY — Liquid Staking Receipt

cCNPY is minted against deposited CNPY at the **current exchange rate** — the very first deposit is 1:1, and thereafter the amount minted reflects how much CNPY already backs each cCNPY. It represents the holder's share of the total pooled CNPY backing.

- **Minting**: Users deposit CNPY via `MessageCanoliqDeposit`, receiving cCNPY at the current rate
- **Redemption**: Users burn cCNPY via `MessageCanoliqRedeem`, queuing a CNPY redemption
- **Claim**: After the unstaking window, users claim matured redemptions via `MessageCanoliqClaimRedemption`

The exchange rate fluctuates as staking rewards accrue:

```
exchangeRate = totalPooledCNPY / totalCCNPYSupply
```

## CPLQ — Governance Token

CPLQ is a **fixed-supply** (100M) governance and value-capture token.

- **Total Supply**: 100,000,000 CPLQ (100,000,000,000,000 uCPLQ with 6-decimal precision)
- **Minting**: Entire supply minted at genesis, distributed across vesting buckets
- **Governance**: CPLQ holders stake for governance weight; voting weight is snapshotted at proposal creation
- **Vote-Escrow**: Locking staked CPLQ for 3–24 months multiplies voting weight (up to 4×) and boosts buyback rewards — see [Vote-Escrow](./vote-escrow.mdx)
- **Vesting**: Allocations follow cliff + linear vesting schedules; recipients must call `MessageCPLQClaimVested` to unlock

### Genesis Distribution Buckets

The full 100,000,000 CPLQ supply is allocated at genesis. Mainnet allocations follow CPLQ
Tokenomics v1.2 §2:

| Bucket | BPS | Share | CPLQ | Vesting |
|--------|-----|-------|------|---------|
| Validators & Infrastructure | 2200 | 22% | 22,000,000 | 12-mo cliff, then 24-mo linear |
| Community & Airdrops | 2000 | 20% | 20,000,000 | 12-mo linear, no cliff |
| Liquidity Incentives | 1500 | 15% | 15,000,000 | 24-mo linear, no cliff |
| DAO Treasury (canoLiq) | 1500 | 15% | 15,000,000 | Not vested — protocol-held, non-circulating |
| Founders & Core Team | 1200 | 12% | 12,000,000 | 12-mo cliff, then 36-mo linear |
| Strategic Partners & Integrations | 1000 | 10% | 10,000,000 | 6-mo cliff, then 12-mo linear |
| Developer Grants & Ecosystem | 600 | 6% | 6,000,000 | Liquid at genesis |

Bucket BPS must sum to `10,000`. See [Vesting](./vesting.mdx) for the schedules in full.

Note that **allocated is not circulating**. Only buckets with no cliff and no vesting are liquid
at genesis — on mainnet just Developer Grants — so genesis circulating supply is **6,000,000
CPLQ**. Vesting allocations enter circulation as they are claimed, and the DAO Treasury tranche
is credited to the protocol rather than a wallet, entering circulation only when governance
spends it.

Localnet and testnet ship shortened schedules; the values above are `genesis.mainnet.json`.
