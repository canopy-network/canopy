# Vesting Schedules

**In one sentence:** most CPLQ is not liquid at launch — 22% of supply waits a full year before
it starts unlocking, and the 15% DAO treasury tranche never lands in a wallet at all.

CPLQ allocations are fixed at genesis and released on vesting schedules with cliff and linear
unlock periods. The total window is `cliff + linear vest`, unlocking linearly between the end of
the cliff and the end of the window.

## Mainnet Schedules

Source of truth: `plugin/go/canoliq/genesis.mainnet.json`, which implements the schedules in
CPLQ Tokenomics v1.2 §6.

| Bucket | Allocation | Cliff | Linear vest | Total window |
|--------|-----------|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Validators & Infrastructure | 22% | 12 mo | 24 mo | 36 mo (3 yr) |
| Community & Airdrops | 20% | — | 12 mo | 12 mo |
| Liquidity Incentives | 15% | — | 24 mo | 24 mo |
| DAO Treasury (canoLiq) | 15% | — | — | not vested — see below |
| Founders & Core Team | 12% | 12 mo | 36 mo | 48 mo (4 yr) |
| Strategic Partners & Integrations | 10% | 6 mo | 12 mo | 18 mo |
| Developer Grants & Ecosystem | 6% | — | — | liquid at genesis |

:::note The DAO Treasury tranche is not a wallet
The 15% treasury bucket is credited to the protocol's own `treasury_cplq` balance, not to any
address. It has no recipients, cannot vest, and **does not count toward circulating supply**.
It is spent only by a passed governance proposal — treasury spends, the buyback, and funding
the [OTC Lock Program](./otc-lock-program.mdx).
:::

:::note Circulating supply at genesis is small
Only buckets with no cliff **and** no vesting period are liquid at genesis, and only those count
toward circulating supply. On mainnet that is Developer Grants alone, so genesis circulating
supply is **6,000,000 CPLQ** of the 100,000,000 total.

Everything else enters circulation gradually: vesting allocations as recipients claim them, and
treasury CPLQ only when governance spends it. (Localnet and testnet show a much larger figure
because they ship several buckets with vesting switched off.)
:::

:::warning Localnet and testnet differ
The bundled `genesis.localnet.json` and `genesis.testnet.json` shorten Liquidity Incentives and
Community & Airdrops to unlock immediately so test networks are usable straight away. The table
above is mainnet. Always check the genesis file for the network you are actually on.
:::

:::note Why the validator cliff is 12 months, not 6
The canoLiq Whitepaper's distribution table (§5.2) once listed a **6-month** cliff for the
validator bucket, while the Tokenomics paper (§2.1 / §6) specifies **12 months** and
explicitly argues a 6-month cliff is *"insufficient given the operational commitment"* required
of validators. The Tokenomics paper governs vesting numbers, and the genesis files implement
the **12-month** cliff. Treat the Whitepaper's 6-month figure as superseded.
:::

## Vesting Model

Each `VestingSchedule` record tracks:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `address` | Recipient address |
| `schedule_id` | Discriminator for multiple tranches per address |
| `total_amount` | Total CPLQ vesting under this schedule (uCPLQ) |
| `cliff_height` | No CPLQ unlocks before this block height |
| `start_height` | Linear vest start (typically equals cliff height) |
| `end_height` | Linear vest completion height |
| `claimed_amount` | CPLQ already moved to liquid balance |

## Unlock Calculation

The `unlockedAmount` function computes cumulative unlock:

- **Before cliff**: Returns 0
- **Between start and end**: Linear interpolation
- **After end**: Returns full `total_amount`
- **Degenerate** (`endHeight <= startHeight`): Returns full amount once past cliff

## Claiming Vested CPLQ

Recipients must call `MessageCPLQClaimVested` to unlock vested CPLQ into their liquid balance:

```bash
canoliqctl cplq-claim-vested <address>
```

The handler reads the `VestingIndex` to find all schedule IDs for the address, then checks each schedule for newly unlocked amounts.

:::note
The FSM does not support range scans. A `VestingIndex` exists per address to enable enumeration without scanning all keys.
:::
