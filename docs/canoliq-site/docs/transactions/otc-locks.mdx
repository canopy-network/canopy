# OTC Locks

**In one sentence:** commit cCNPY for a fixed 90- or 120-day term and collect a one-off CPLQ
reward when it matures, while the cCNPY keeps earning staking yield the whole time.

The OTC lock program is aimed at buyers who are happy to hold cCNPY for a set period. You give
up the ability to redeem for the term; in exchange you receive CPLQ on top of the yield your
cCNPY was already earning.

:::info Added after the v1.2 papers
This program is not described in the canoLiq v1.2 Whitepaper or Tokenomics paper. It was
specified separately and shipped afterwards; both papers are pending an update.
:::

## The two tiers

| Term | Reward | You get back |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 90 days | 5% of the locked amount, paid in CPLQ | All of your cCNPY, plus the reward |
| 120 days | 8% of the locked amount, paid in CPLQ | All of your cCNPY, plus the reward |

The reward is a straight quantity conversion: lock 50,000 cCNPY on the 90-day tier and you are
promised 2,500 CPLQ. There is no price feed involved, so the reward you are quoted when you
lock is exactly the reward you receive.

**Minimum position: 50,000 cCNPY.** Anything smaller is rejected.

## What to know before you lock

- **Your cCNPY is not redeemable during the term.** It leaves your spendable balance and sits
  in the lock position until you close it.
- **It keeps earning.** Locking does not remove your cCNPY from the staking pool, so it goes on
  appreciating at the normal exchange rate for the whole term. The cCNPY you get back is worth
  more CNPY than the cCNPY you put in.
- **Your reward is set aside up front.** When your lock is accepted, its CPLQ is reserved out of
  the program budget immediately, so a matured position is always payable. If the budget cannot
  cover a new position, that lock is rejected rather than accepted and underfunded.
- **Leaving early costs you the whole reward.** You get every cCNPY back, but no CPLQ.
- **Once matured, you must claim, not cancel.** Cancelling a matured position is refused, so a
  mistimed command cannot destroy a reward you have already earned.

## Locking

```bash
canoliqctl otc-lock <nickname> <ccnpy-amount> <90d|120d>
```

Amounts are in micro-cCNPY, so the 50,000 cCNPY minimum is `50000000000`.

```bash
canoliqctl otc-lock alice 50000000000 90d
# otc lock submitted: tx_hash=… from=… ccnpy=50000000000 tier=90d
```

Note the lock id from the resulting position — you need it to close the lock. It is also listed
under `otcLocks` on `GET /v1/account/{addr}`.

## Claiming at maturity

Once the term has elapsed, claim to collect both sides:

```bash
canoliqctl otc-lock-claim <nickname> <lock-id>
```

This returns your cCNPY to your spendable balance and credits the CPLQ reward. A claim submitted
before maturity is rejected.

## Cancelling early

```bash
canoliqctl otc-lock-cancel <nickname> <lock-id>
```

Your cCNPY is returned in full and the CPLQ reward is released back to the program budget for
someone else to claim. This is irreversible: the position is gone, and re-locking starts a fresh
term.

## Fees

| Action | Fee |
|--------|-----|
| `otc-lock` | `stake_fee` |
| `otc-lock-claim` | `claim_fee` |
| `otc-lock-cancel` | `claim_fee` |

All default to 10,000 uCNPY and are governance-configurable.

## Not the same as a vote-escrow lock

canoLiq has two lock systems and they do different things:

| | OTC lock | [Vote-escrow lock](../tokenomics/vote-escrow.mdx) |
|---|---|---|
| What you lock | cCNPY | CPLQ |
| Terms | 90 or 120 days | 3, 6, 12, or 24 months |
| What you get | A one-off CPLQ reward | Multiplied voting weight and a bigger buyback share |
